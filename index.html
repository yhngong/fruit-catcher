📖 How to Play

⏱️
60-Second Time Attack!

You have exactly 60 seconds to score as many points as possible. Beat the clock and set a record high score!

🍎
Catch Falling Fruits

Apples, Oranges, Bananas, Strawberries, Watermelons, and Pineapples award points and coins!

💣
Avoid Bombs! (-50 Pts & -3s)

Catching a bomb costs -50 points, inflicts a -3 second penalty, and resets your combo multiplier.

⚡
Combos & Fever Mode

Catch fruits in a row without missing to build multipliers up to 5x. Filling the fever meter unleashes a Golden Fruit Rain shower (with a 5s cooldown between fevers)!

🧲
Power-Ups

Catch Magnets (pulls nearby fruit), Clocks (+5s bonus time & slow-mo), and Shields (blocks 1 bomb).
