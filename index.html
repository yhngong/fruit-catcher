📖 How to Play

🍎
Catch Falling Fruits

Apples, Oranges, Bananas, Strawberries, Watermelons, and Pineapples award points and coins!

💣
Avoid Bombs!

Catching a bomb costs 1 heart and resets your combo multiplier.

⚡
Combos & Fever Mode

Catch fruits in a row without missing to build multipliers up to 5x. Filling the fever meter unleashes a Golden Fruit Rain shower!

🧲
Power-Ups

Catch Magnets (pulls nearby fruit), Clocks (slows time), and Shields (blocks 1 bomb).
