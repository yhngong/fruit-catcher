📖 How to Play

⏱️
2 Minutes & 30 Seconds Time Attack!

You have 2 minutes and 30 seconds (150s) to score as many points as possible. Beat the clock and set a record high score!

🍎
Catch Falling Fruits

Apples, Oranges, Bananas, Strawberries, Watermelons, and Pineapples award points and coins!

💣
Avoid Bombs! (-3s Time Penalty)

Catching a bomb inflicts a -3 second penalty and resets your combo multiplier (no points lost!).

🔥
Hard Mode (Double Bombs & Double Coins!)

Switch to Hard Mode for double bomb frequency, simultaneous double-bomb drops, and 2X COINS! Warning: Fever mode does NOT protect you from bombs in Hard Mode!

⚡
Combos & Fever Mode

Catch fruits in a row without missing to build multipliers up to 5x. Filling the fever meter unleashes an 8-second Golden Fruit Rain shower (in Normal mode, fever grants complete bomb invulnerability; in Hard mode, watch out for bombs!).

🧲
Power-Ups

Catch Magnets (pulls nearby fruit), Clocks (+5s bonus time & slow-mo), and Shields (blocks 1 bomb).

🎵
Dynamic Music & Sound Effects

Procedural chiptune background music shifts dynamically with Normal mode, Hard mode, and Fever Time! Toggle music with 🎵 (or B key) and sound effects with 🔊 (or M key).

🎼
Music & Sound Credits

Original procedural chiptune soundtrack and Web Audio synthesizer engine created by Antigravity (Google DeepMind) & Yolet Gong. Synthesized in real time via the Web Audio API with zero external audio files.
